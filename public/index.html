How it works

From one line of text to a post-ready reel

Each step hands structured data to the next, so you can stop, edit anything, and carry on.

  1. 01Idea

    Claude suggests topics from trends, the season, a topic bank and how your past reels did. Or type your own.

  2. 02Script

    Written beat by beat as JSON: the spoken line, the words on screen, the layout, the images and the motion.

  3. 03Writers' room

    A second Claude pass scores the script, lists every problem and rewrites it until it passes.

  4. 04Voice

    Text-to-speech with timings for every word, so the words on screen land exactly when they're spoken.

  5. 05Images

    Pulled from your tagged library or generated, with the background removed and the colour checked.

  6. 06Design and motion

    Each beat gets a layout, type, palette and animation, then it's drawn frame by frame.

  7. 07Sound and render

    Sound effects on the visual hits, music ducked under the voice, mixed to Instagram loudness.

  8. 08Self-check

    Claude watches contact sheets of the finished video, fixes what's wrong and rebuilds.

Features

Everything a reel needs, in one place

✍️

A writers' room, not one shot

A reviewer scores the hook, specificity, proof, retention, flow, accuracy, visuals and the call to action, then sends the script back for a rewrite. Your own notes count as top-priority fixes.

🎙️

Word-synced voice

Hinglish or English voices from Sarvam or ElevenLabs, or the free macOS voice. Numbers and tricky words are made speakable first, and audio is cached so rebuilds are free.

🖼️

A smart image library

Models, garments and props are tagged and reused. Missing ones are generated, cut out and recoloured to an exact hex. A reference photo keeps the same person across every pose.

🎬

A motion design system

Kinetic type lockups, colour wipes, photo cards, flows, giant words behind a model and letter fly-throughs. Motions are plugins you can preview and rate.

🔍

It checks its own work

After rendering, Claude looks for text over faces, cut-off or unreadable words, overlaps, empty frames and wrong colours, then fixes them before you ever see the reel.

🎓

Learn from any reel

Upload a reel you love. The engine measures its cuts, colours and sound hits, and Claude writes a style card. New reels borrow its ingredients without copying it.

📈

Learns from your results

Connect Instagram to pull views, saves, shares and watch time for every reel. Those numbers shape the next round of ideas.

🎞️

Finish it anywhere

Every reel comes with a cover, caption, subtitles and voice track. Export layered tracks and an .fcpxml timeline straight into DaVinci Resolve.

Get started

Up and running in two minutes

Needs Node 20+ and ffmpeg. Works best on macOS.

# clone and install
git clone https://github.com/DeepakKohlii/ai-reel-engine.git
cd ai-reel-engine
npm install

# start, then open http://localhost:4747
npm start

FAQ

Questions

Do I need to pay for anything?

No. Without keys it uses Claude Code (or claude.ai copy/paste), the free macOS voice and a free image generator. Keys only make the voice and images better.

Which Claude do I need?

Any of three: an Anthropic API key, the Claude Code CLI logged in with your Claude subscription, or plain claude.ai, where the studio gives you the prompt to paste.

Is it only for fashion?

The built-in formats and prompts are tuned for men's style reels, but the pipeline (script, review, voice, images, motion, render, self-check) is general. New formats are folders in src/formats/.

Does it post to Instagram for me?

No. It makes the reel, cover and caption, and you post it. With Instagram connected, it reads each reel's stats to plan better ideas.

Can I edit what it makes?

Yes. Every field of the script is editable in the studio, there's a raw JSON editor, and you can swap any image. Only what you changed is rebuilt.
