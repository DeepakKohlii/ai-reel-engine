How it works

From one line of text to a reel you can post.

Every step hands structured data to the next, so you can stop anywhere, edit anything, and carry on.

  1. 01

    Idea

    Claude suggests topics from trends, the season, a topic bank and how your past reels did. Or you type your own.

  2. 02

    Script

    Written beat by beat as JSON: the spoken line, the words on screen, the layout, the images and the motion.

  3. 03

    Writers' room

    A second pass scores the script on eleven things, lists every problem, and rewrites it until it passes.

  4. 04

    Voice

    Text-to-speech with a timing for every word, so the words on screen land exactly when they're spoken.

  5. 05

    Images

    Pulled from your tagged library or generated, with the background removed and the colour checked.

  6. 06

    Design and motion

    Each beat gets its layout, type, palette and animation, then it's drawn frame by frame.

  7. 07

    Sound and render

    Sound effects land on the visual hits, music ducks under the voice, and the mix hits Instagram loudness.

  8. 08

    Self-check

    Claude watches contact sheets of the finished video, fixes what's wrong, and rebuilds.

A writers' room, not one shot

A reviewer scores the hook, proof, retention, flow, accuracy and more, then sends the script back for a rewrite. Your own notes count as top-priority fixes.

Stop wearing brown with black.

Words land on the voice

Every word has a timestamp, so type appears exactly when it's said. Hinglish or English, via Sarvam, ElevenLabs or the free macOS voice.

A library that grows

Models, garments and props are tagged and reused. Missing ones are generated, cut out and recoloured to an exact hex.

text over face → fixed

It checks its own work

After rendering, Claude looks for text over faces, cut-off words, overlaps and empty frames, and fixes them before you see the reel.

style cardtype · palette · cuts · sound

Learn from any reel

Upload a reel you love. It measures the cuts, colours and sound hits, and new reels borrow its ingredients without copying it.

~script~*serif*#CONDENSED#!sticker!%note%

A motion design system

Kinetic type lockups, colour wipes, photo cards, flows, giant words behind a model and letter fly-throughs. Motions are plugins you can preview and rate.

Learns from your results

Connect Instagram to pull views, saves, shares and watch time. Those numbers shape the next round of ideas.

Finish it anywhere

Cover, caption, subtitles and voice track come with every reel, plus layered tracks and a timeline for DaVinci Resolve.
